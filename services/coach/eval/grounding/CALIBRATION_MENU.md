# Calibration entry menu — you pick the representative set

The first 20-case set wasn't representative: dups (one entry 4×, another 3×) + 2 stubs
the judge hallucinated a story for. Root cause: the old sampler picked *claims*
(3–6 per entry), not *entries*, and never filtered stubs.

This menu fixes that at the source. **You choose which entries are representative.**
Below: a recommended **18** (one claim per entry, all distinct, no dups, no stubs,
covering all 10 themes), plus a swap-in pool, plus the full 45-entry catalog.

## How to use this

- Toggle `[x]` ↔ `[ ]` to add/drop an entry.
- Net target: **18–20** entries checked.
- When done, reply **"go"** (or tell me your cuts/swaps in chat).
- I rebuild the sampler with a `--slugs` mode → generates exactly your set,
  one claim per entry. You then re-label a clean 18–20.

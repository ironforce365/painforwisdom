## Definition
The core belief that "if I'm ordinary I'm worthless" — a wound that drives overachievement, perfectionism, and the need to be exceptional. It manifests as an inability to rest, compulsive goal-setting, and equating self-worth with output. Current work involves recognizing when this wound is driving behavior versus genuine motivation.

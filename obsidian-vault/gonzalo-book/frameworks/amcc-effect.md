# AMCC Effect

**First appeared:** 2026-02-17
**Last updated:** 2026-02-24

## Definition
The Anterior Mid-Cingulate Cortex grows when you do things you do not want to do — voluntary discomfort builds override capacity. This is the neuroscience behind why choosing hard things (not just enduring them) strengthens your ability to override future resistance.

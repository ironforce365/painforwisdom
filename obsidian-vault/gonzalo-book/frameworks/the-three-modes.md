# The Three Modes

**First appeared:** 2026-02-23
**Last updated:** 2026-02-23

## Definition
Three states of operating: cruise control (autopilot, going through the motions), rigid structure (forcing discipline without awareness), and conscious presence (deliberate, aware engagement with what you are doing and why). The goal is to spend more time in conscious presence.

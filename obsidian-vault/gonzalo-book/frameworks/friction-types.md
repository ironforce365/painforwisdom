# Friction Types

**First appeared:** 2026-02-18
**Last updated:** 2026-02-24

## Definition
Three categories of friction: growth friction (the resistance that comes from doing something genuinely hard that develops you), system friction (obstacles created by poor planning, logistics, or environment), and pattern friction (resistance that comes from old habits or identity patterns fighting against change).

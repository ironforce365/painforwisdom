# Comfort as Default

**First appeared:** 2026-02-18
**Last updated:** 2026-02-24
**Entry count:** 2

## Core tension
Why do capable people default to easy, and what does that drift look like from the inside? This is not about crisis or collapse -- it is about the quiet, daily pattern of choosing the shorter workout, the safer conversation, the path of least resistance, and the slow softening that follows.

## Key insight so far
The person most at risk is not the one in crisis but the one in drift -- and the exit from drift is smaller than they think. Every postponed email, deferred errand, or avoided conversation is both a symptom of comfort-as-default and an opportunity to override it. The aMCC doesn't care about the size of the task; the act of overriding avoidance is the rep. Gonzalo's practice of sitting without his phone and forcing action on whatever he's postponing is the daily antidote to drift.

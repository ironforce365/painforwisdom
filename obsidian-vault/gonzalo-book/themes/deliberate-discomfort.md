# Deliberate Discomfort

**First appeared:** 2026-03-02
**Last updated:** 2026-03-13
**Entry count:** 2

## Core tension
When does choosing the harder path serve genuine growth versus when is it ego-driven suffering? How do you train yourself to invert the default optimization toward ease?

## Key insight so far
Growth hides inside the conditions most people optimize away. The deliberate choice to seek harder conditions when easier ones are available is what separates strategic discomfort from passive training. But showing up in those conditions does not require performing at full intensity — the act of choosing the hard environment and staying in it, even at reduced effort, is itself the training. The aMCC cares about the override, not the split time.

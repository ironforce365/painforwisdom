# Preparedness Debt

**First appeared:** 2026-02-18
**Last updated:** 2026-02-18
**Entry count:** 1

## Core tension
What is the real cost of comfort? Not stagnation but active debt -- a growing deficit in capacity that remains invisible until a moment of forced reckoning arrives and you discover you have no reserves to draw on.

## Key insight so far
Always choosing the easy route does not keep you safe -- it creates a preparedness debt that comes due the moment life demands something hard. The 12-hour ultra exposed this: Gonzalo was not unprepared because the race was too hard, but because his daily defaults had never built the override muscle.

## Core tension
Can you learn to tell the difference between your body saying "stop" and your mind negotiating a surrender -- and does that skill transfer to the moments that matter most?

## Key insight so far
Recovering under load teaches you more than building toward a peak. By frontloading the hardest session to Monday, Gonzalo turned the rest of the week into a diagnostic lab -- the point was not the hard session itself but what he could learn from observing his body and mind in its aftermath. The skill being developed is signal literacy: building a personal fatigue dictionary that distinguishes genuine physical limits from mental negotiation.
